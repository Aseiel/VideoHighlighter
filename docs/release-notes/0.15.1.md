# VideoHighlighter 0.15.1

The theme of this release is timing. Subtitles now appear when the words are said, not up to half a second before, and they can be lined up with the cuts the way subtitlers time them. The app can hand a video's shot changes to a subtitle editor as an EDL. The Whisper model you pick is the one that runs. And search gets "more like this moment".

5 commits on main since 0.15.0.

## Known in this release

- **The shot list uses the simpler detector in this build.** The learned shot-boundary model (TransNetV2) is not downloaded by Setup yet, so cuts are found from frame differences. That finds most hard cuts, and fewer than the model does on fast, animated footage. Running from source with `models/transnetv2/` in place (`tools/export_transnetv2.py` makes it) uses the model.
- **Search in other languages is still English-first on Windows.** The model that reads queries in your language is not among the published packs yet. The macOS build carries it.
- **"Download and install" is not offered yet.** Only a build that carries the release signing key offers it, and this one does not.

## Subtitles

- **Lines land where they are said.** On an Intel GPU, Whisper's line times over a long stretch fell on a half-second grid, 0.1-0.5 s before the speech. Each line now takes the times of its own words, with the text unchanged. On a 21-minute episode the median start error went from 0.32 s to 0.06 s.
- **Time subtitles to shot changes.** A new option under Subtitle Settings, off by default. A line that starts within half a second of a cut starts on it, and one that ends near a cut ends just before it, so text and picture change together. On the same episode, lines starting on a cut went from 17 to 115, and lines running a few frames into the next shot from 55 to 12.
- **No more missing stretches.** When Whisper locked into a repetition loop at the start of a 30-second window, the rest of that window's speech was dropped with the loop; one episode lost 30 seconds of dialogue. The stretch is now decoded again from a little later.
- **The model you pick is the model that runs.** "Make subtitles" and "Scan transcript" ran the Whisper model saved by the last full run, not the one in the dropdown.

## Shot changes

- **Export shot list (EDL).** Right-click a video in the list: the app finds every cut to the frame and writes `<video>_shots.edl` (CMX 3600) and `<video>_shots.txt` beside it, the formats subtitle editors import as shot changes. No sensitivity to tune.
- **"Scene" is now "shot" where it means the cut.** What the motion scan finds is a shot change; a scene is several shots. Settings and caches keep their old keys, so nothing needs re-running.

## Search

- **More like this moment.** Right-click a moment you know is right and choose "More like this moment in the whole video": the video is ranked against the moment itself instead of against words, which is often what a vague word needed. Results are places, not single frames. Over MCP too.
- **Indexing reads in order.** A scan that wants most of a stretch reads the video front to back instead of seeking to each frame: about 2.5 minutes instead of 7-9 for a 76-minute film.

## Timeline

- **Zooming is smooth on long videos.** The time ruler redrew the whole film on every wheel step; it now draws what the view can reach. Fifty steps on a 76-minute film went from about 27 s of blocked window to under 2 s.
- **The filmstrip fills faster.** Thumbnails come from the keyframe inside each slot instead of an exact seek: thirty slots over a whole film in 1.0 s instead of 3.6 s.
