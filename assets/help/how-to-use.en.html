How to use VideoHighlighter

Give it a long video, get back the moments that matter, then finish the edit here or in your own editor. Everything runs on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

1. Quick highlight

The start screen (Simple view) does a whole run with one button.

  1. Drop a video onto Drop a video here, or click it to choose a file.
  2. Choose a Highlight length: Short (about 1–2 minutes), Medium (about 4) or Longer (about 7).
  3. Press Analyze. The first run on a video takes longest; later runs reuse what was already found.
  4. When it finishes, Open timeline shows the picked moments, and Open report explains each one.

The finished highlight video is saved next to the original.

2. Detailed settings

Press Detailed settings… on the start screen for every option. View → Simple view takes you back.

  • Add Videos fills the list at the top. Several videos can run in one go.
  • Basic Settings: how long the highlight is and what to look for.
  • Transcript & Subtitles: turn on speech-to-text, so what is said can count, and write subtitles.
  • Advanced: detectors, scoring and the highlight report.
  • Presets: save your settings under a name and load them later.

Then, at the bottom:

  • Run Highlighter analyses the videos and renders the highlight.
  • The arrow next to it has Score only (no video): it re-scores with the current settings and writes the report, without rendering a video. It is fast on a video already analysed, so it is the quick way to try a setting.
  • Tick Export clips to also save every picked moment as its own file, in a _clips folder next to the highlight.

3. Edit the timeline

Timeline Viewer shows the whole video with everything that was found: speech, faces, objects, motion and scene cuts. Below it is the edit timeline with the picked clips.

  • Play, trim and delete clips, or add your own with Add Clip.
  • Render Highlight Video writes the edited cut as a video.
  • Export hands the cut to another editor: EDL or FCPXML (DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, Final Cut), CSV, or a CapCut / Jianying project that appears straight in CapCut's project list.

4. Why a moment was picked

Highlight Report opens a page that explains every picked moment: what scored it, what was detected, what was said, and the good moments that just missed the cut.

AI Summary adds a few plain sentences to that report: what shaped this cut and the one change most likely to improve it. It uses a local AI model, so it takes a moment. The button next to it (the wheel) has more: read what happens in the cut, ask a question, or choose the model.

AI Summary needs a local model. The LLM Chat tab sets one up.

5. Teach it something new

VideoHighlighter finds what you show it. If it does not know the thing you care about, teach it:

  1. Open the Train tab.
  2. Give it a few videos with examples of the thing, and check the samples it sorts.
  3. Train. The new model is used on the next run.

Community models… on the Advanced tab lists models other people have made and shared.

6. Questions

It is slow.

The first run on a video does the detection; later runs reuse it. A graphics card helps a lot: NVIDIA, AMD and Intel are all supported.

The language is wrong.

View → Language. It changes after you restart the app.

Something went wrong.

Help → Open debug log file opens the full log. Send it with your question.
