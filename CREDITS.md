# Thanks

People outside the project whose requests and reports made VideoHighlighter
better. The app lists them too, under About → Thanks
(`modules/ui/credits.py` keeps the two lists the same).

## Scott Napier

- **Shots.** Asked for a frame-accurate shot list for subtitling. The result
  was the shot list exported as EDL, found by a learned shot-boundary model:
  436 shots in a 20-minute episode, in 46 s.
- **Subtitles.** Asked for subtitles timed to shot changes. On the same
  episode, lines starting on a cut went from 17 to 115, and lines running
  into the next shot from 55 to 12.
